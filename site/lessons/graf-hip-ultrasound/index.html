Graf hip ultrasound: learn it on one image

An interactive teaching brief built on a real Graf standard-plane image. Work through steps 1–12 beside the image: each step changes what is drawn on it. It is a study aid, not a substitute for supervised scanning.

Image: Walter SG et al., Front Pediatr 2022;10:990806, Fig. 2 (cropped; in screen view rotated and flipped so that cranial is on the left), CC BY 4.0. The original authors' printed labels and arrows were removed digitally (inpainted); faint smoothing may remain where they were. All labels now come from this page. The coloured overlays are added here: landmark positions are hand-placed approximations, and the simulation in step 12 is a schematic drawn over the image, not a real dysplastic scan.
